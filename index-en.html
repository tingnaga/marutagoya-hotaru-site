MARUTAGOYA HOTARU  —  Log Cabin in the Satoyama of Naka City

CONCEPT

Wrapped in the warmth of wood and the quiet of the satoyama,
a special time with the people who matter most.

Marutagoya Hotaru is a log cabin set in the satoyama countryside of Naka City, Ibaraki Prefecture. Your stay includes exclusive use of both the log cabin and an adjoining single-story building. Set well apart from neighboring homes, the spacious grounds open onto a garden surrounded by rural scenery. Whether it's a three-generation trip, a family summer holiday, or a gathering with close friends, large groups can relax and spread out, surrounded by nature.

Up to 20 guests (3 bedrooms) Exclusive use of 2 buildings Spacious garden BBQ & outdoor hearth Quiet, set apart from neighbors

FACILITY

Facility

A wood-scented log cabin paired with an adjoining single-story guest building. A free, open layout that only a whole-property rental can offer, comfortable even for large groups.

Exclusive use of 2 buildings ・ Up to 20 guests

Enjoy exclusive use of both the log cabin and the single-story building. With 3 bedrooms , the spacious layout suits large groups and three-generation trips alike.

A spacious garden

Nearby is a biotope pond where you can encounter seasonal creatures and plants while strolling. Set well apart from neighboring homes, it offers plenty of privacy too.

BBQ & outdoor hearth

The garden has BBQ equipment plus a simple outdoor hearth. Gather around the coals, or try your hand at hearth cooking.

Firefly viewing (seasonal)

Surrounded by nearby waterways and rice paddies. On early-summer evenings, you may catch sight of fireflies around the property.

Nagashi-somen (optional)

Try nagashi-somen, noodles flowing down a bamboo flume — an available option for a summertime treat with family or friends.

Stone bathtub with hinoki interior

A generously sized stone bathtub beneath a fragrant hinoki (cypress) ceiling and walls — a wonderfully relaxing way to soak away travel fatigue.

NATURE

The Charm of Nature

Satoyama countryside that changes its expression with each season. Here you'll find nature experiences you won't find anywhere else.

Seasonal ・ Early summer

Fireflies at Play, a Gift of Summer Nights

Along the waterways around the property, fireflies can sometimes be seen dancing on early-summer nights. Turning off the lights and waiting quietly is, in itself, one of the luxuries unique to the satoyama.

  • Best viewed roughly early to late June
  • Sightings depend on weather and temperature and are not guaranteed
  • A popular nature-watching experience for children and grandchildren alike
Optional experience

Nagashi-somen: a Summer Tradition on a Bamboo Flume

We can set up a bamboo flume in the garden for nagashi-somen — noodles flowing down a split bamboo channel. It's a classic summer activity that gets everyone, from kids to adults, involved.

  • Reservation required (please request when booking)
  • Setup sized for large groups
  • Pairs well with a BBQ
Year-round

Breathe in the Satoyama Air

The property is surrounded by rice paddies and coppice woods. Nearby is a biotope pond you can stop by while strolling, home to seasonal creatures and plants. Set apart from neighboring homes, mornings greet you with the calls of bush warblers and pheasants, while evenings let you have the relaxed satoyama air all to yourself.

Before requesting a booking

Please normally request a direct booking at least 28 days before check-in. Requests within 28 days remain open to individual consultation, subject to availability and preparation. Existing stays and their three-day buffers remain unavailable. A request is not a confirmed booking; only our confirmation establishes the reservation.

Tax included: JPY 28,000 per night for up to four guests, plus JPY 4,800 per additional guest per night. Total = (28,000 + 4,800 × guests beyond four) × nights. Adults and children pay the same. Bedding type or use does not change the accommodation price. Separately requested experiences or options may incur additional charges, quoted before application.

Maximum 20 guests. Sleeping arrangements depend on group size and composition; two guests may share a double bed.

Check-in from 15:00; check-out by 11:00. Please ask in advance about early check-in; we accommodate requests when preparation allows.

Direct bookings: cancellation is free through eight days before check-in; 100% from seven days before through check-in day. Final guest count is normally set seven days before check-in. Reductions after final confirmation are not refunded. Increases require individual approval of capacity, bedding and operating conditions, with a recalculated price (maximum 20). The property pays bank fees for refunds. OTA bookings follow their agreed conditions.

Full payment by bank transfer is normally due seven days before check-in. For an exceptionally accepted booking after that deadline, we specify an individual payment deadline. We may cancel an unpaid reservation following individual review; cancellation is not automatic. Guests pay the bank transfer fee for accommodation payments.

Children accompanied by an adult are welcome. Groups consisting only of minors cannot stay. Do not admit unregistered visitors to the property without prior permission. Illegal activities, conduct against public order or morals, and use for providing or receiving sexual services are prohibited. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, use may be stopped and guests may be asked to leave.

No pets (assistance dogs are handled in accordance with law). No indoor smoking, including electronic cigarettes. No Wi-Fi. Parking comfortably accommodates about four cars; ask about more. Use BBQs and fire only in designated areas, never on artificial turf. Clean equipment and dispose of charcoal and ash in designated places. Outdoor fireworks: courtyard only, until 22:00; no aerial fireworks.

Enjoy the changing seasons in our natural surroundings. Insects may occasionally enter rooms, and spider webs may appear outdoors or around the buildings. We clean and maintain the property with care, but cannot completely prevent these features of the natural environment.

Specific check-in instructions and emergency contact details for your stay will be provided privately before arrival.
